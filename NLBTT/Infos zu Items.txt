## **ItemManager.cs** - Complete Item System

### Key Features:
1. **All 11 Items from the PDF** with German names:
   - Taschenlampe (Flashlight)
   - Hasenstatue (Bunny Statue)
   - Messer (Knife)
   - Nadeln im Glas (Jar of Needles)
   - Getrocknete Libelle (Dried Dragonfly)
   - Altes Brot (Old Bread)
   - Haufen Asche (Pile of Ashes)
   - Krähenfeder (Crow Feather)
   - Bärenkralle (Bear Claw)
   - Notrationen (Emergency Food)
   - Obsidiansplitter (Obsidian Shard)

2. **Inventory Management**:
   - Maximum 3 items
   - Player starts with Flashlight
   - Press **'I'** to toggle inventory UI
   - Console logging with `LogInventoryToConsole()`

3. **Item Effects Implemented**:
   - **Passive effects**: Crow Feather (+2 stamina cap)
   - **On destruction effects**: All items with destruction bonuses
   - **Trigger effects**: Knife bonus bloodpoints, Pile of Ashes doubling, Old Bread on damage, Emergency Food on food gain, Obsidian Shard auto-resurrection

4. **UI System** (similar to EventUIManager):
   - Inventory panel showing all items
   - Item descriptions in German
   - Close button
   - Current count display

5. **No Debug Toggle in Inspector**: All debug logging is always active (no serialized field to disable it)

### Integration with Player:
- ItemManager is attached to the same GameObject as Player
- Player notifies ItemManager of:
  - Health loss (for Old Bread, Pile of Ashes, Obsidian Shard)
  - Food gain (for Emergency Food)
  - Bloodpoint gain (for Knife, Pile of Ashes)
- Flashlight cooldown decrements each turn

### Setup Instructions:
1. Add the **ItemManager** component to your Player GameObject
2. Create an **Inventory UI Panel** in your Canvas with:
   - TextMeshProUGUI for title
   - TextMeshProUGUI for item list
   - Button for closing
3. Assign the UI elements in the Inspector
4. Apply the Player.cs updates I provided

The system is ready to use! Press 'I' in-game to view your inventory. Items will automatically trigger their effects when appropriate conditions are met.
